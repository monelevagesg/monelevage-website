You host the tasting.
It does the counting.

The Tasting Companion Lite is a scoring tool for one evening. Your guests open a link on their own phones, score each wine as you pour, and the totals add themselves up.

No app to download. No spreadsheet. No sitting in the kitchen with a pile of paper while everyone else drinks.

How it works

  1. You set it up Add your wines in the order you're pouring them. Takes about ten minutes. Not sure what order to pour in? My free blind tasting guide has a prompt that works it out for you.
  2. Your guests join from a link They put in their name and start scoring. Nothing to install.
  3. You close the scoring when you're ready Guests can't see the results until you decide it's time.
  4. You read out the PIN The room sees the ranking, and every score behind it.
