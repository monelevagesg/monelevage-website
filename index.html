The Beginning

Two decades at 35,000 feet

Twenty years with Singapore Airlines. Wine became my specialism, I took WSET Level 3, and spent my last eight years on board as an air sommelier. Serving wine at altitude teaches you fast that the experience around the glass matters as much as what's in it.

On the Floor

Sommelier and operations manager

I worked the floor at a private members club in Singapore. I built the cellar list, hosted the tastings, and ran the nights that bring people back. It's also where the gap showed up clearly: good wine programmes are held together by spreadsheets and memory.

The Build

So we built it

My husband has spent years shipping digital products at MNC scale. Together we built the members platform we needed. Not a pitch deck. A working product, built from the floor up.

Mon Élevage exists because we saw the gap and closed it ourselves.
Now we do it for others.

The Tasting Companion

Everything about the wine,
in their hand.

Guests join from their phone with a link and a PIN. The wines, the notes, the producer background, all in one place they can look at before, during and after the tasting. They score as they go, the results reveal when you're ready, and a keepsake PDF lands in their inbox afterwards.

Built for sommeliers who host tastings, and for importers, distributors and suppliers running trade sessions. Nothing to download, nothing to print.

Guests join by link and PIN, no app needed
