How it runs

Four steps,
start to finish.

01
You send the wines

A list is enough. We handle the flights, the notes, the producer background and the running order.

02
We set it up

Pages, scoring, PINs and a QR code, ready to print for the table or drop into a group chat.

03
Guests taste and score

They read, taste and rate on their own phone. No app, no signup, no fumbling. Guest note taking can be switched on if you want it.

04
Results and keepsake

You reveal the scores when the moment is right. Everyone gets a PDF of the night afterwards.

No handouts. No app.
Just the phone already in their hand.

Two ways to run it

Take the tool,
or take the night off.

Same tool underneath. The difference is who stands up and runs the room.

Option one

The tool,
you host

You run the tasting. We build everything behind it and hand you the keys before the night. Good for sommeliers with their own crowd, wine clubs, and trade tastings where the wines change every time.

  • Your wines, your flights, your notes
  • Live scoring and results
  • QR code and PINs ready to go
  • Keepsake PDF to guests afterwards

From SGD200 per event, all in.
Two events, SGD300.

Option two

The tool,
we host

Everything above, plus a sommelier on the floor running it. I build the flights, write the questions, lead the tasting and read the room. You get to be a guest at your own event.

  • Flight design and input on the wine selection
  • Blind tasting questions written for your crowd
  • Sommelier hosting on the night
  • Post-event report and guest keepsake

Price on request, based on length, group size and how much we build.

Recurring programmes and larger events are quoted separately. If you run tastings every month, tell us and we'll price it properly instead of charging you like it's a one-off.
