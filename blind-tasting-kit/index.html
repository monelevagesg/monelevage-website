How to run a blind tasting at home

At a BYO dinner everyone can see the labels. The expensive bottle gets the compliments, and nobody learns anything.

Cover them and the night gets interesting. The compliments go to whichever wine earned them, and you find out what you like with no name or price telling you what to think.

This is the simplified version of the method I use when I run tastings professionally, written for a dinner table.

  • The prompt I use to sort wines into flights, ready to copy into your AI tool of choice
  • A question bank that works whether your guests know wine or don't
  • How to score it, and how to run the reveal so it lands
